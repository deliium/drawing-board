# Practice hiragana lines still not real kana shapes

**Date:** 2026-09-11 00:20
**Files:** content/hiragana5/v1/traces.json, content/hiragana5/v1/strokes.json, content/hiragana5/v1/manifest.json, content/hiragana5/v1/review.json, content/hiragana5/LICENSES.md, web/src/curriculum/hiragana5-traces.json, internal/recognize/testdata/hiragana5/**, README.md
**Severity:** medium

## Problem

After hand-tuned densify/reshape, practice stroke-order and trace guides still did not look like real hiragana (wrong topology / approximate silhouettes), especially お.

## Root Cause

Project-authored control polylines invented a simplified お (vertical + separate right loop) and other approximate forms. Densifying those paths could not invent schoolbook stroke topology.

## Solution

- Replace UI traces and assessment strokes with paths sampled from [KanjiVG](http://kanjivg.tagaini.net) schoolbook SVGs (`03042`–`0304a`), normalized to unit space (お = horizontal, vertical-into-loop, top-right tick).
- Refresh gold/incorrect fixtures + `contentHash`.
- Document CC BY-SA 3.0 attribution for the geometry files in `LICENSES.md`.

## Prevention

- Prefer a schoolbook stroke dataset (e.g. KanjiVG) over freehand approximate polylines for learner-visible guides.
- When changing stroke topology, update assessment templates and fixtures together; record third-party license/attribution.
